PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Mixer Toolbox provides free audio engineering calculators and reference tools. This policy explains how website usage information and advertising-related data may be handled.

ANALYTICS

Mixer Toolbox uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall site traffic and usage. Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed to measure website activity without using cookies or tracking individual visitors across websites. Cloudflare may process technical information needed to provide this service. Learn more in Cloudflare's Web Analytics information.

We also use Google Search Console to understand how the site appears and performs in Google Search. Search Console provides search performance and indexing reports, rather than an individual visitor analytics profile.

ADVERTISING

Mixer Toolbox has integrated Google AdSense and may display advertisements provided by Google if approved. When Google's advertising services are active, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to display and measure ads, and, where permitted, personalize them.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at Google's partner sites information page. You can manage Google ad personalization preferences through My Ad Center.

PRIVACY CHOICES

Where required, we will provide choices about advertising-related data use through Google's consent and privacy messaging tools. Those choices may vary by region. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

CONTACT

For questions about this policy, contact hello@mixertoolbox.com.

UPDATES

We may revise this policy if our services or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
